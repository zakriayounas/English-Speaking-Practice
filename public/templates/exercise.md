Question:
Your teammate asks, "Want to lead the standup?"
You were late. What do you say?

A: Sorry I'm late. I'll keep this short.
B: Whatever, just start without a plan.
C: You should have waited for me.
D: I have nothing to add, honestly.
Answer: A
Explanation:
"Sorry I'm late" takes responsibility without stopping the meeting.
**I'll keep this short** shows you respect everyone's time.

Use this when you still want to contribute.

Question:
A colleague says the release "slipped".
What does that usually mean?

A: The release moved to a later time.
B: The release was cancelled forever.
C: Someone dropped the server.
D: The team finished early.
Answer: A
Explanation:
In software teams, **slipped** means the date moved later.
It does not mean the work was thrown away.
